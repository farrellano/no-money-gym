# Why I Created NoMoneyGym

It all started with a very simple problem: **working out with saved Instagram videos was a nightmare**.

## The Problem

Whenever I was training, I'd watch exercise routine videos on Instagram. I'd find an exercise I liked, save it, and then go to my saved videos section to use it during my workout.

The problem is that Instagram wasn't built for that. Here's what kept happening:

### 📱 The screen would turn off

You're in the middle of an exercise, watching the video to follow the movement, and suddenly... the screen goes dark. You have to unlock your phone, find the video again, and pick up where you left off.

### ⏭️ The video ended and another one started

You're doing an exercise, the video finishes, and automatically a completely different video starts playing. Now you have to pause, go back, and find the one you need.

### 🏠 Instagram sends you to the home feed

This was the worst one. You're watching a saved video, you swipe to the next one, and for some reason the app throws you back to the main feed. Now you have to navigate back to your saved videos, find where you were, and hope it doesn't happen again.

## The Idea

After dealing with this a thousand times, I thought: **why not save the video directly and mark the start and end of the part I actually need?**

That way I could:

- Have the video saved in one place
- Trim just the exercise portion I need
- Build a circuit with multiple exercises
- Work out without any interruptions

## The Solution: NoMoneyGym

That's how NoMoneyGym was born. An app where you can:

1. **Save your exercise videos** — Download them from anywhere and upload them to the app
2. **Trim the start and end** — Select only the active portion of the exercise
3. **Build circuits** — Combine multiple exercises with work and rest intervals
4. **Train without interruptions** — The screen won't turn off, videos won't skip, and everything is centralized

## Everything in One Place

The core idea is simple: **centralize everything in an app designed for training**. No more depending on Instagram, no more screen frustrations, no more losing track of your routine.

And the best part: it's free, works offline, and your videos never leave your device.
