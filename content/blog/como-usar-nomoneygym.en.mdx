# How to Use NoMoneyGym: Step-by-Step Guide

NoMoneyGym is a free web app that lets you create Tabata and circuit training routines using **your own videos**. Everything works offline and your data never leaves your device.

## How does it work?

Watch this visual tutorial showing the complete app flow:

![NoMoneyGym Tutorial](https://u5tm2hrh6dxrgqkg.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/nomoneygymhowwork.gif)

## Step 1: Upload your exercise videos

Record your own exercise videos or download them from wherever you prefer. Then, from the **Exercises** section, tap **"+ New"** and select the video from your device.

## Step 2: Trim and classify

Once the video is uploaded, you can **trim** the active portion of the exercise. Select the start and end points so only the part you need plays. Then, give it a name and assign a **muscle group** (legs, chest, back, etc.).

## Step 3: Build your circuit

Go to the **Circuits** section and create a new one. Select the exercises you want to include, set the **work and rest** intervals, the number of **rounds**, and you're done.

## Step 4: Start training!

Hit play on your circuit and train with a timer that guides you automatically. It includes optional **voice cues** so you don't have to look at the screen.

## Key Features

- 🆓 **100% free** — No subscriptions, no payments, no trial
- 📴 **Works offline** — After the first load, no internet needed
- 🔒 **Complete privacy** — Your videos stay on your device
- 🎤 **Voice cues** — Tells you when to work and rest
- 💾 **Backup** — Export and import your data anytime
